# Rally School Online: approved YouTube lesson sources

Last reviewed: 2026-10-11

This roster is intentionally selective. Keep a channel here only when it has both credible coaching credentials or a substantial, corroborated coaching record and enough publicly accessible, clearly instructional video to support lesson cards with captions, timestamps, and visual demonstrations. Inclusion is not blanket endorsement of every video or technical claim.

## Editorial rules for lesson creation

- Search by skill and learner level, then approve the exact video—not the whole channel—for that lesson.
- Prefer a clear court demonstration, usable captions, stable timestamps, and specific explanation of what to do and why.
- Build cards from the transcript and visible demonstration. Preserve coach attribution and conditions such as contact height, incoming pace, grip, and player level.
- Compare consequential or disputed technique claims across at least two credible sources. Coaches may use different cues for different players; don't present one cue as universal without support.
- Link to and timestamp the original video. Use brief excerpts only when appropriate. Prefer original diagrams for action flows; use frames only when they add clear teaching value.
- Subscriber counts, charisma, testimonials, commercial claims, and elite-player associations can provide context but do not validate an individual technical claim.

## Not in the approved roster

- **Total Tennis Domination** and **Tennis University** did not meet the current evidence bar for verified coaching credibility during this review. Reconsider only if stronger credential evidence is found and a useful public lesson is screened.
- **Feel Tennis** may be a useful supplementary teaching source, but the credential evidence reviewed so far was not strong enough for this high-credibility roster.
