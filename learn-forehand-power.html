TAKE IT TO THE COURT

Watch, rehearse, then test

  1. Watch the video’s “Practicing stroke mechanics” chapter and pick one movement the coaches demonstrate.
  2. Rehearse that movement slowly for five shadow swings. Keep the range comfortable and balanced.
  3. Ask a coach or practice partner to feed ten easy balls. Compare your movement with the video, then stop if the cue makes your contact less controlled.

This is a Rally School practice task built around the video’s practice chapters, not a drill quoted from the coaches. Juniors should try technique changes with their coach.
