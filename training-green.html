🟢 GREEN BALL TRAINING

Three-Player Full-Court Session

A 60-minute practice for two Green Ball players plus Ethan as an active hitting partner. All three players stay involved through cooperative rallies, 2-on-1 patterns, movement drills and competitive point play.

⏱ 60 Minutes 👥 3 Players 🎾 Green Ball 📍 Full Court 📈 Intermediate
SESSION GOAL

Consistency → Movement → Patterns → Serve → Compete

This session uses Ethan as an active hitting partner rather than only a feeder or observer. The main structure is player-to-player hitting and 2-on-1 patterns so all three players get frequent repetitions while working on consistency, movement, direction, transition, serve-and-return patterns, and point construction.

Optional Skill Focus · Green Ball

Forehand Extension to a Deep Target

Use this for 3–4 minutes near the end of the drill, once players are moving well and finding a comfortable contact point.

Purpose

Help a player send a controlled forehand deeper by continuing the racquet forward through contact, rather than stopping or wrapping the swing across the body too early.

How It Works

  • Place a large target in the deeper half of the opposite court.
  • Use a cooperative crosscourt feed or rally ball.
  • Player adjusts, turns, contacts slightly in front, and extends smoothly toward the target.
  • Rotate after 6–8 controlled forehands each.

Coaching Cues

“Bend and turn.” Keep the knees softly flexed and make a unit turn.

“Relax, then send.” Keep the hand relaxed in preparation; let the racquet travel through the target.

“Stable at contact.” Keep the racquet face steady at contact, then finish balanced and recover.

Avoid: forcing the elbow above the shoulder, trying to push the shoulder past a specific foot, squeezing the grip too hard, or swinging harder just to create depth. The goal is a smooth, controlled ball toward the deep target.
